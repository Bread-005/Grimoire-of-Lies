Grimoire of Lies

Grimoire of Lies is a solo social deduction game. You take on the role of an all-seeing observer: you have access to all information in the game, but must interpret it correctly to determine what is true and what is false.

A number of players (controlled by the game) are seated in a circle. Most players belong to the Good team, while a smaller number belong to the Evil team. Each player is assigned a unique role, which provides them with specific information or abilities.

All players claim to be a good role, regardless of their true alignment.
- If a player is Good and not affected by conditions such as being drunk or poisoned, their information is accurate and their ability functions correctly.
- If a player is Evil, the information they present is false, and their abilities do not behave as they claim.

As the observer, your task is to analyze all available information, track inconsistencies, and determine each player's true identity.

Your goal is to eliminate the Evil players - most importantly, the Demon.
- If you successfully eliminate the Demon and no Demon remains in play, you win.
- If the game reaches a state where only two players remain alive and one of them is the Demon, you lose.

Success depends on your ability to distinguish truth from deception in a system where both can appear equally convincing.
